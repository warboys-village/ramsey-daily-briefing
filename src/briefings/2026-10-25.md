Before Peterborough became synonymous with the East Coast Main Line, the city's first railway station was Peterborough East. Opened in 1845 by the Eastern Counties Railway, it served routes towards Northampton, Rugby, March, Wisbech and Norwich. The station was known simply as Peterborough until being renamed Peterborough East in 1923.
